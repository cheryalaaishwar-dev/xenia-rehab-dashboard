# Xenia Physio & Rehab Center Dashboard

A comprehensive, responsive healthcare patient treatment tracking, rehabilitation monitoring, and clinical management dashboard.

## 🚀 Overview

**Xenia HMS Dashboard** is an enterprise-grade rehabilitation and physical therapy web application featuring real-time clinical telemetry, modular healthcare workflows, therapist scheduling, live session stopwatch telemetry, and guardian communication portals.

---

## 🛠️ Modules & Features

1. **Executive Dashboard & Analytics**:
   - Patient intake statistics and ongoing vs completed treatment metrics.
   - Interactive Chart.js visualizations (Monthly registrations, treatment status distribution, progress trajectories, and therapist workload).
   - Dynamic time-range filters (*Today*, *This Week*, *This Month*, *This Year*).

2. **Module 1: Patient Registration & Family Linking**:
   - Centralized patient directory with verified identification cards.
   - Guardian contact directory with authorized consent linkage.

3. **Module 2: Clinical Assessment & Treatment Planning**:
   - Comprehensive clinical assessments, baseline functional measures (ROM, VAS pain scoring), and personalized rehabilitation programs.

4. **Module 3: Therapist Allocation & Scheduling**:
   - Specialist assignment, appointment slot booking, recurring sessions, and double-booking conflict detection.

5. **Module 4: Live Treatment & Session Tracking**:
   - Interactive live therapy stopwatch (Start, Pause, Resume, Complete).
   - Real-time progress bar telemetry, VAS pain rating slider, and clinical observation recording.

6. **Module 5: Treatment Progress & Goal Monitoring**:
   - Longitudinal progress tracking, initial assessment score vs current score comparisons, and milestone completion percentages.

7. **Module 6: Family Communication & Notifications**:
   - Authorized guardian dispatches via SMS, Email, In-App Push, and WhatsApp.
   - Medical disclosure consent verification and automated appointment reminders.

8. **Module 7: Patient Condition & Therapist Reports**:
   - Longitudinal condition diagnostics (Physical, Emotional, Mobility metrics), treatment efficacy ratings, and follow-up timelines.

9. **Module 8: Family Dashboard & Patient Monitoring**:
   - Secure, read-only guardian portal providing transparency over patient recovery without altering medical records.

## 💻 Tech Stack

- **HTML5 & Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)**
- **Tailwind CSS** (via CDN)
